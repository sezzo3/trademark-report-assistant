Privacy

Reporter settings are saved in chrome.storage.local on this browser profile, not synced by the extension. Browser extensions do not provide encrypted secret storage; use a trusted device.

Report URLs, evidence and review confirmations stay in popup memory until it closes. Clicking Fill draft fields sends the entered reporter and case information to the official platform form in the active tab. The platform can read information entered into its form before final submission; its own privacy policy applies.

There is no dashboard connection, analytics, external font loading or automatic network request. Open official form navigates to the selected platform. No report is submitted automatically.

Use Clear saved reporter data to remove local settings and any legacy extension data. This does not erase fields already entered on a website or reports previously submitted.

Permissions: storage saves reporter settings; activeTab identifies the form tab after a user action. Content scripts run only on the specified official trademark report paths to fill ordinary fields on request. No host_permissions, scripting permission or page-message bridge is used.
